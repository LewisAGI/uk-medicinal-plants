# Suggested features: uk-medicinal-plants
- Evidence badge on every use (colour + text: Clinical / Mixed / Traditional only / No known benefit / Not endorsed) with "why" drawer showing the basis and source.
- "Is this safe to touch/eat?" toxicity lookup with RHS category and an always-visible "If poisoned: call 999 / NHS 111, take a plant sample" panel.
- Lookalike comparison cards side by side (hemlock vs cow parsley, foxglove vs comfrey/mullein, wild garlic vs lily-of-the-valley), with a "never forage on an app ID" warning.
- Medicine-interaction warnings surfaced first for St John's wort (contraceptives, antiepileptics, antidepressants).
- Filter: native/wild vs garden, by grade, by toxicity.
- "Ask a pharmacist" prompt and THR-mark explainer; no dosing content, enforced by a content lint rule.
- Myth vs evidence section built from clinical_mixed entries (ginkgo, echinacea, garlic, milk thistle).
